# Sales-Analysis-Dashboard
# 🤖 AI & ML Jobs Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
The **AI & ML Jobs Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive dashboard built entirely in **Microsoft Power BI**. It provides insights into the AI & Machine Learning job market by analyzing salary trends, job locations, hiring companies, experience levels, remote work opportunities, and the most in-demand technical skills. The dashboard enables users to explore the data through interactive visualizations and filters.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze AI & ML job market trends.
- Compare salary trends across different experience levels.
- Explore job opportunities by country and city.
- Identify top hiring companies and job roles.
- Analyze remote work opportunities.
- Discover the most in-demand AI & ML skills.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Power BI Data Modeling
- Interactive Visualizations

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1️⃣ Overview
The Overview page provides a high-level summary of the AI & ML job market using interactive KPI cards and charts.

**KPIs**
- Total Jobs
- Average Salary
- Average Salary by Experience
- Median Salary
- Remote Job Percentage

**Visualizations**
- Monthly Job Posting Trend
- Experience Level Distribution
- Jobs by Experience Level
- Country & City Salary Analysis

---

### 2️⃣ Location Insights
This page analyzes job opportunities across different countries and cities, helping users understand geographical hiring trends.

**Visualizations**
- Region Filter
- Country & City Treemap
- Filled Map
- Country-wise Average Salary
- Region, Country & City Matrix

---

### 3️⃣ Company & Role Insights
This page focuses on hiring companies, job roles, company ratings, and salary comparisons.

**Visualizations**
- Company-wise Job Distribution
- Job Title Analysis
- Company Rating Analysis
- Remote Ratio vs Salary Bubble Chart

---

### 4️⃣ Skill & Hiring Insights
This page highlights the most demanded technical skills in AI & ML job postings and their relationship with salaries and experience levels.

**Visualizations**
- Skill Usage by Experience Level
- Average Skill Usage
- Job Title vs Average Salary
- Skill vs Salary Treemap
- Experience-wise Skill Distribution

---

## 📈 Key Insights
- The dashboard analyzes more than **10,000 AI & ML job postings**.
- The average salary is approximately **93.4K USD**.
- The median salary is **102.5K USD**.
- Senior-level professionals generally receive higher salaries.
- Remote jobs represent around **10%** of total job postings.
- Python, SQL, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, NLP, and Cloud Computing are among the most in-demand skills.
